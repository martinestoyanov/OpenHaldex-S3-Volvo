Volvo P1 DEM

Volvo S40 / V50 / C30 Haldex couplings carry a Volvo DEM that speaks Volvo's 29-bit HS-CAN matrix, not VW frames. This page edits the frame profile the controller generates toward the DEM in standalone mode, shows what the DEM reports back, and lets you send single frames on the DEM bus while bench testing. Select Volvo Gen 2 or Volvo Gen 3 in Setup first. Read docs/volvo-p1.md before enabling frames.
